Anyone coming to Havalla looking for a fresh start in life will be pointed towards this building. The Library of New Beginnings stands at the heart of the city towering high over the roofs of common man, impossible to miss.

{% quote "A passer-by pointing out the Library of New Beginnings to his friend" %}

See the church tower over there? That's the Church of Renewal. You can find books there about every profession and religion, from entry level content to very advanced techniques and philosophy. There's something for everyone there who's looking for something new. And if you're really stumped and don't know what you're looking for, go ask the librarians! They're a great help.

## Burning Fire of Passion
The Library of New Beginnings is the epicentre of profession passion in Havalla. The librarians of Sacred Knowledge have extensive knowledge about various professions, making them very capable sparring partners when looking for a change in career. Inside, the walls are covered with books, neatly categorised by profession. Cosy benches, lounge chairs, and tables fill up the floor providing readers with a place to sit back and have a look at their stack of books.

On the outside, a large tower serves as the gateway to a new life. It looks similar to that of a church with the bells clearly visible and the pointy roof sticking out over the city. The rest of the building blends in with the city.

{% secret %}

### Recruitment Ground
The librarians of Sacred Knowledge carefully navigate their way through the slew of people looking for a change. This library is the perfect setup for them to recruit new members for the organisation. They make it a priority to getting to know the people coming in looking for a new life. What are their interests, what kind of change would they like to see, what is their background.

If they feel like the person could be persuaded to join the organisation, they will try to plant a seed of doubt. If their perception of life is challenged, they are more willing to accept a given solution. It is a labour intensive process and a long one too, but in the end it is worth it in most cases.

{% endsecret %}

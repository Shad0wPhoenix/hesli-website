Illtresi is one of the younger kingdoms in [Eshil](/atlas/eshil). Rising to power only a few centuries ago, this kingdom is known for its generosity. They have successfully helped out numerous cities and towns throughout the region. In return, these places joined the kingdom sharing their organisations and resources.

{% quote "An officer encouraging his troops" %}

Alone, we might be weak, but together we can withstand everything!

{% endquote %}

## Generosity
The kingdom has helped out various cities and towns. Usually with troops, or to give an extra hand during negotiations. They were especially helpful when Matona was having issues with the [Haldir](/encyclopaedia/ethnicities/haldir) over the use Zedonias. A spokesperson helped the Matona spokesperson with the negotiations. When these negotiations turned sour, the army swooped in to prevent the Haldir from accessing the remaining Zedonias. They also were the reinforcements during the Haldir Frenzies, and proved vital in driving back the Haldir into the mountains of Greze Urngorr.

When [Limani](/atlas/eshil/limani) was in a tough pickle with the Grilashi Empire, pinned down at the banks of the Nacheilchomor, they answered the call of the town with an immense army. Together with Kali Gerno and the remainder of the town's resistance, they drove the Grilashi Empire beyond the Rudeilemor and well into Bani Reshumba.

## Goverance
The kingdom is known for its lenience in governing their subjects. As long as they bring something to the society, all is well. For example, Blacksmiths make weapons and armour as a ways of paying tax. Professions are therefore capable of providing the government with their wares as a ways of paying tax. Other organisations, especially merchant organisations such as the Limani Supplies Dealer usually pay their taxes simply in the form of money.

Most of this money is used to provide their subjects with residency. Making sure everyone has a home is important to the kingdom. Another chunk is used to fund the subjects in their education. Those wanting to switch careers, or wanting to attend full time academy. This way, the kingdom hopes to make sure everyone has an equal chance to find a decent paying job and therefore securing the well-being of their people.

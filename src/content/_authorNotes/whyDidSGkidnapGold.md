**What did really happen?**
The mortal species started to think more for themselves and, in that process, started to form more communities with like-minded people - often of their own species. Eventually, they asked their respective dragon masters if they could help them create a village and how to build houses. They also asked for more free time from their duties, so they can bond more with the people in their communities.
The dragons happily taught them how to build homes, but decided to let them learn on their own as well. They only told them how to build a house, but not which material would be best to use. This way, they hoped to further stimulate these peasants. To compensate for the reduced workforce, the dragons created more of the peasants kin to compensate; for each peasant is created by its master and therefore is under its spell and listens to every command given by its master.
However, to some dragons, this was an absolute infringement on their power over the peasants. They were, however, very much a minority; so much so that other dragons simply shooed them away and didn't take them serious. Over time, these begrudged dragons found company in like-minded brethren leading to the founding of the Splinter Group.

Over time, the mortal species started to form families. They even started to procreate! They become more independent, asking for more free time from their duties to focus on their growing families. This is a turning point for the dragons because since their peasants can reproduce, they get less easily influenced by their commands.
This turns the mood among dragons sour, as more dragons proclaim the wish of the Splinter Group: to eradicate the mortal species! As more dragons get annoyed and shaken by their lack of power and influence, they learn about the Splinter Group causing it to grow. They gain little more influence because they're still a big minority, but more and more influential dragons join their cause. They don't need numbers, they need dragons with influence to join their cause.

The communities of the mortal species grow even further. The stimulation of the dragons early on by letting them learn what materials are best for building a house, accidentally laid the foundation of the mortal species individual thinking and learning how collaboration could make them achieve things they never could alone.
As the mortals keep on procreating, the dragons start to lose their grip over them entirely. New generations become less susceptible to their command and magic. So where they could control the first generation with maximum absoluteness, the second generation did mostly okay, and the third started to get out of their control from time to time. But with each generation passing, their influence dwindled until the newest generation of mortal couldn't be influenced and commanded by them anymore.
That doesn't mean that the world goes under; the mortal species still fulfil their duties to and with the dragons. Many of the dragons believe this is the path evolution should take and still actively encourage their subspecies to venture further into the world outside of their reach. They should be able to stand on their own feet and navigate what the world has to offer. After all, what use is maintaining a world if there's no one to explore it?
However, to some, losing control over the species is the final straw and the Splinter Group is a big one. It is this moment that they start to look for a "solution" to the "problem".

**What is the solution according to the Splinter Group and how does Gold fit in the picture?**
According to the Splinter Group, the world should return to the dragons. Although they do see the huge benefit of having the mortal species doing a lot of the dirty work, they believe that these mortals should be firmly under dragon control with no way of escaping - that would lead to their death.

They believe that Gold them the mortals the individuality, the way to think for themselves. Therefore, they believe that by pulling Gold out of the equation, everything will return "back to normal".

**Are they aware that Gold has a sister?**
Yes, but they believe her to be weak. Gold has always been the vocal and present one. Silver is more shy and stays in the background. But as we all know; dogs that bark the loudest don't bite...

The Splinter Group believes that removing Gold grants them their wish, therefore they abduct her and bring her to another pane of existence, so she cannot influence the world of Hesli any longer... => Maybe the Realm of Eternal Slumber?
However, the abduction is the trigger to start the war. Someone cannot find Gold, a few weeks later they hear the brags of the Splinter Group and that kicks off the war.

! Actually, it is the combination of Silver and Gold that "caused" the mortal species to start thinking for themselves !

**What did the Splinter Group leave behind that could be found in present day?**
Teleporting a dragon to another pane probably requires a portal. The Splinter Group probably found a way to build the portal and those schematics haven't been completely destroyed in the war. That said, it's most likely written in an ancient language, so it'd be difficult to decipher.

**What could be a reason for the dragons to return? Why did they leave in the first place?**
After the war, the remaining "good" dragons were treated the same as the Splinter Group by the mortal, who came to know that they are the cause of the entire conflict. Their vision of and about the dragons has clouded. Therefore, the remaining dragons decided to leave the planet in the hands of the mortals.

The few members of the Splinter Group that survived went into hiding, either in the world or in the Realm of Eternal Slumber. There, they tried to keep Gold contained, but she managed to escape their grasp. The Splinter Group learns from this pane that the world remained unchanged or rather, it turned even worse! From the Realm, they try to find a way back and reunite with the hidden members in the overworld.

By the time the campaign starts, they are on the cusp of finding a way to communicate from the Realm to the overworld, which then sets off new events that result in the return of the dragons to Hesli.

**What is Hesli's actual origin story?**
In the very beginning, there was nothing. Dragons from another land stumbled upon this rock floating in space. Why they were there to begin with, nobody knows. But those dragons, colourful in scales, saw the rock and imagined a home.

So they began to use their magic on the rock. The red-scaled dragons manipulated the magma and the earth, creating land and mountains. The green dragons populated the earth with trees, vegetation and animals. The white dragons introduced water to hydrate everything, but also to create vast and unreachable ecosystems - like the oceans. The blue dragons made the air easily breathable and created the weather. Finally, the Black dragons brought with them death and destruction, for there needs to be someone that creates balance between the old to make room for the new.

After a long time of creating and adjusting, these dragons could finally call this place their home. Still, they needed to maintain it by themselves, which was doable, but far from ideal. Especially tasks that required finer motor skills were the bane of the dragons with their large claws.
So, to help them out, they created mortal subspecies in their image. The red dragons created the Dvares, stocky and short creatures capable of immense strength and endurance and with an intense focus on community and loyalty. The green dragons created the Aileeves and taught them that to nurture, they must first understand. The white dragons created these small creatures they called Geenoms. These creatures have immense lung capacities that allows them to stay under water for longer. The Blue dragons created featherlight small creatures that would never be bound to the ground; they taught their Hälvi that the horizon isn't a boundary; it's an invitation to explore and discover the unknown. The Black dragons created the final subspecies; a species so squishy yet incredibly lethal without them even knowing. "Hoomans kill by curiousity" is what the Black dragons say to describe them, rather endearingly.

For a long time, the dragons and the mortal subspecies lived in harmony. They maintained the world and made it beautiful. But there were never visitors. The mortal subspecies did as they were told by their masters, and their masters never left their side.
That was until two visitors arrived: the sisters Gold and Silver. Gold was the loud and boisterous one, and fiercely protective of her sister. Silver was more shy and quiet, but also highly intelligent. They were wandering the universe in search of a new place to settle down when they found Hesli. They brought new knowledge to the world in the form of ambition and intellect.
As Silver and Gold help the dragons and their mortal subspecies to reach new heights, In that process, the dragons encouraged the mortal subspecies to start thinking for themselves. However, this caused the dragons to fracture internally as they started to lose their grip on the mortal species.
In response a Splinter Group formed and eventually kidnapped Gold.

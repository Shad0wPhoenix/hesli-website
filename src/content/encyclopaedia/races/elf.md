Reaching the top of branches with ease and feeling right at home amongst the trees, the animals and the mushrooms - the elves of Hesli have found their calling: to nurture all that lives. Everyone looks up to them for guidance whenever one feels stuck, for few dedicate themselves to understanding others as deeply as the elves do.

{% quote "The Opening Quote of the Rite of Understanding" %}

It is time to spread your wings and fly, my yngly. For to do your duty and nurture all that is life, one must first learn how to stand where day stand.

{% endquote %}

From an early age the elves are taught in various ways to see the world through another's eyes before offering guidance of their own — a philosophy that has earned them a reputation as patient listeners and trusted advisors.

## Reaching New Heights
In tales of old, elves are described to have been born from the very essence that is life. Some people even believe that the elves emerged from the trees surrounding the [Livetsträd]().

{% quote "A Curious Daughter" %}

Mum, mum, is it true that we come from The Tree? Is that why we are so tall with long arms and legs?

{% endquote %}

During the ancient span of elven tribes, its members adapted to life amongst the forest canopy. Their limbs, hands, and feet gradually became longer to make it easier to reach the uppermost leaves while their torsos remained relatively unchanged. Counterintuitively, the elves have mastered the art of balance even with their higher center of grativy.

While they have trouble navigating in the dark of the caves, the elves still have excellent sight which shines in darker forests. They can distinguish a multitude of green and brown colours, making it nearly impossible for imposters to hide within the bushes.

## Where to Find Them
Most [High Elves]() surround themselves by the meadows and plains with the odd forest here and there. They thrive among the animals and food-bearing plants making sure everyone has enough to eat. Few find refuge close to the mountains to help cave-dwelling species flourish.

The [Wood Elves]() live their lives in the vast forests that covers the ground as far as the eye can see. These elves barely touch ground, so much so that some have forgotten entirely how to walk just on their legs.

Finally, the [Swamp Elves]() inhabit the place where water meets land turning it into swamps and marshes. Their closer connection to nature and life makes them the wisest of all elves and also avid teachers of their crafts.

## Speedrun to Adolesence
Elves grow rather quickly. A child will be fully grown by the age of 10 and have learned to speak fluent Elvish by that time. From age 10 till age 30, the final accentual parts in Elven biology will develop, therefore becoming a full adult at the age of 30.

{% aloud %}

#### Taboo
Beyond 30, it becomes forbidden to speak of or ask about elven age, especially from the olden days. Currently, their average lifespan is around 375 years, but this used to be 750 a long time ago. According to [The Records of Eilmaris](), in ancient times, someone had a condition that resulted in halving their life expectancy. Long before it was discovered, the condition had spread genetically resulting in only a few elven bloodlines staying out of this horror.

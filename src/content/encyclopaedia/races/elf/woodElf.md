Wood Elf

You're asking the hard questions, aren't you? What would someone have to understand when they first meet us. Well, as you can see, we're never alone, really. A wood elf without its companion animal is like... a human without a beating heart. Okay, maybe not _that_ dramatic, but losing your companion.. I cannot express the grief I feel. And it's different too; it really depends on the circumstance. If it's of old age and you've already phased over to a new permanent companion, it's such a different kind of grief then when your companion dies in battle or dies in vein.. the latter is the worst, honestly.. No, no, I don't want to talk about it further, it hurts too much, I'm sorry.

I see you noticed. Yes, we have very flexible arms and legs and we hardly touch the ground if we can help it. You'll find our homes up in the trees; we often laugh at new bibi's trying to find us. Bibi's? Oh right, of course, you wouldn't be familiar with that word, bibi is what we usually call friendly non-wood-elfs that actually take a genuine interest in us. Truly, especially outside the bigger settlements, if a wood elf brings you home, expect to be part of the family now. Also, don't try to keep up with our antics in the trees.. you'll hurt yourself, we wouldn't want that.

Oh right, I drifted off, sorry about that. That squirrel over there is just.. taunting me. What were you asking again? Oh right, a normal day for us wood elfs. Well, there's the endless quaffle we have with the high elfs. They may say that all is well and good but really.. well, depends on who you ask on both sides, honestly. My grandfather absolutely would throw me out the trees into the pond if he'd see me fraternising with a high elf. That said.. he has been coming around. There are a few of them that are properly angry at their ancestors for what they did to us. Oh, you don't know? Well.. them high elves chased us out of the World Tree. We took to the water because they get their weird waterfrost whenever they touch impure water; what a nuisance. But yeah, we got driven from our home because "oh my gawd this tree so pretty we must have it for ourselves". Okay okay, I'll stop the mocking voice, sorry. Oh wait, I still didn't tell anything about our normal day, my bad, let me see. Well, we tend mostly to the trees and the birds and all animals that take shelter under the leaves. We prefer making our way through the trees rather than taking the path underneath but for some things the path is easier, I must admit.

Also, if you want to befriend a wood elf extra quickly and make a good impression on the elders; find yourself some perfectly ripe zingerberries and turn it into the greatest zingerpies in existence. That ought to get yourself the attention of every wood elf. Granted, there's a decent chance your pie won't survive once it gets to the village, unless you have it protected by other wood elfs. Just.. a heads-up. Me? Hell yeah, I love me a piece of zingerpie, you got some?

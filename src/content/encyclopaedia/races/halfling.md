No race is as fearless and spontaneous as the halflings; they soar through the sky unafraid of lightning and wind. They are taught from a young age that the horizon isn't a boundary but an invitation. To stand beneath the open heaven is to understand that the world is vast, and there is always another path to follow. The way they go about it is as chaotic as can be: grab what's needed for survival and just.. go.

{% quote "Rite of the Skies" %}

Follow it.

{% endquote %}

Halflings are driven by pure joy. To them, the greatest stories are the ones nobody has written yet, or seeing a place no one in their family has ever seen before. The biggest compliment one can get from a halfling, is being taken to _their_ place; a spot they haven't told a soul about. Many halflings keep these places for years, returning to them whenever the world grows too loud.

## Uncontainable
Ask a halfling to stay in one place for too long and even the strongest walls begin to feel like a cage. Trying to keep a halfling in one place rarely ends well. Sooner or later, they will find a way out. Their keen instincts and eyesight give them everything they need to become Hesli's quickest escape artists.

That said, halflings do not mind staying in the same place for an extended period of time as long as it's of their own volition. Some prefer the freedom of having a singular place to come back to whereas others always want to be on the move. The invitation will always arrive exactly when it's time for a new adventure.

Which is exactly why it is impossible to say where to find halflings. They are literally everywhere in the world. From the highest mountain peak to the wettest swamp, if a halfling feels a calling to a place they will make their way there. Novelty, discovery, or simply the chance to lend a helping hand may keep a halfling somewhere for days, months, or even years. Yet sooner or later, the horizon calls again.

## The Royals of Dramatic Differences

"Make sure the wind doesn't just pick you up!" A young human sneers.

"At least I know I'll land where I want to be without a stratch!" The young halfling laughs.

"Tibbe, mind your manners! We taught you not to joke like that," Tibbes mother quickly scurries over. "This youngling has seen more of the world than most of us ever will in all our lifetimes combined. Ask for his stories instead instead of making fun of him."

{% endaloud %}

Of all the sapient species, no subspecies differ more dramatically from one another than the halflings. Among the three of them, they share but two physical traits: they are short and light. This isn't all that strange considering just how much of their lives they spend in the skies. Most races love to joke about their featherweight but halflings happily prove the joke right, gladly letting the wind carry them wherever it pleases.

Encountering a Skye Halfling travelling alone is rare — and usually cause for concern. These halflings spend so much of their lives together with their mount that many outsiders struggle to tell where one ends and the other begins. From the moment the younglings learn to fly, a mount will choose one as their companion for life.

Watching a Wind Halfling take off is common, but watching one land is another matter entirely. These halflings seem to spend more time in the clouds than upon the ground unless they have found a new spot to settle for a while. They love to circle their environment to map and discover everything there is to see.

If the other halflings flirt with danger, the Lightning Halflings are practically married to it. They are the truly fearless ones. They have a reputation for treating thunderstorms as just another fun rollercoaster. Using their magic, they ride the lightning reaching such immense speeds that it's a borderline wonder their bodies still hold together. They're the only ones who wear the iconic mad-scientist haircut the best of all peoples of Hesli.

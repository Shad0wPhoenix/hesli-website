Give them an impossible task and the only question in response is "How long do I have?" Humans believe everything is possible, as long as you put your mind to it and have the funds to do so. The great variety in thinking, curiousity, and ambition gives them one undeniable trait: humans are highly unpredictable.

## Mentality
Humans have never been particularly gifted. They are neither the strongest - for the dwarfs are -, the wisest - that's for the elves -, the swifest - the halflings got them beat there -, or the most resilient - that's gnome territory - and they are most certainly not the longest-lived. Yet, they leave their mark on nearly every corner of Hesli.

Where others see a mountain, humans wonder what lies beyond it. Where others accept tradition without question, humans ask whether it could be done differently. It is this relentless ambition — equal parts inspiring and dangerous — that has shaped their history more than any gift they were born with.

While other species mock them for their short lifespan, humans take that as fuel to do what they do best: remain as unpredictable as ever. They try to cram as much life as possible into the years they have, making them more prone to reckless decisions in pursuit of their passions.

## Where to Find Them

Few species adapt to their surroundings as readily as humans. Rather than allowing the land to shape them, humans have an uncanny habit of shaping themselves to fit the lives they wish to live. As long as there is life or something to discover; humans will naturally flock towards those places.

Humans seldom settle where their bodies are most comfortable; instead, they settle where their ambitions and preferences can flourish. If a human prefers the cold, they're more likely to live in a colder region than than a tropical one.

Umbra humans prefer the shadier parts of town, both literally and figuratively. Their natural talents as guardings from the shadows thrive most in these conditions.

The squintic humans are often split in two; there are those who love working in a lab indoors and only come outside when strictly necessary. Then there are those who prefer explorign all there is to discover in the outdoors. They will climb over mountains just to see what's on the other side.

The mind humans prefer living among other people, no matter what race. As long as they can obtain some form of influence over time, these humans will happily live in both large and small communities. These humans crave a larger social purpose.

## Versatile Physiology
At first glance, humans appear remarkably unremarkable. They often have an average build, but don't stand out too much from the rest of the species. That being said, their physiological range is surprising.

Humans come in many shapes, sizes, and levels of intellect. They are much more varied than any of the other sapient species. Some are tall, others broad-shouldered. Some possess remarkable intellect, while others seem born with endless endurance. For some reason unknown to current scholars, humans are excellent as a control group when experimenting with medicine to find cures to conditions or diseases.

A lifetime of physical labour can produce a broad, powerful build reminiscent of a dwarf, while years devoted to study may leave another human pale and slender. Their bodies readily reflect the lives they choose to lead. The versatility of their body also allows them to make changes to their appearance with relative ease by changing their lifestyle. This makes humans extremely useful in adapting to new environments and tasks.

# Welcome in Hesli, Curious Bean!
Hesli is a world filled with adventure and cosy/wholesome vibes, where not everything is quite as it seems: concealed meanings, unsuspecting twists, and dangers hide in plain sight waiting for the right opportunity to strike.

## So, where do we kick off?
Many people start their adventures in Limani, [Eshil](/atlas/eshil) - a bustling region filled with a wide variety of places, biomes, people, and cultures to explore. [Limani](/atlas/eshil/limani) is one of the prominent hubs in this region. As a key point in the trading network - with people flocking to the city to become part of the Limani Supplies Dealers - it has become this melting pot of ethnicities and traditions.

If you're looking for some work, be sure to find [Ashish Ludak](/npcs/ashishLudak) or immediately head to [the Wandering Lileon](/atlas/eshil/limani/wanderingLileon) to speak with the owner [Barnabas Stripepaw](/npcs/barnabasStripepaw). Otherwise, feel free to explore the town or the nearby villages!

## Companions

Are you alone and looking for work to get your life as an adventurer started? Be sure to check out the local pubs and quest boards, surely you can make some friends there to start an adventuring company. Or maybe you fancy a life working for one of the organisations that allows you to travel the world.

## What's going on?
While Eshil may look peaceful on the outside, there are plenty of forces active within trying to suppress a grim reality.

### A Covert Brotherhood Stirring the Pot
Spend enough evenings in the taverns of [Limani](/atlas/eshil/limani) and you'll hear the whispers of a secret underground organisation pulling strings from the shadows. No one knows what their intentions are, but it's clear that this organisation has a political agenda.

On the road, you hear rumours among merchants that the Limani Supplies Dealer is working together with the Duskdealer. Together, they hope to uncover the truth and put an end to whatever it is this brotherhood seems to be plotting.

### A Terrifying Prospect in Theorston Thicket
If rumours are to be believed, people have begun disappearing at a steady rate since the past couple years. However, in the past few months, the rate has tripled and it's causing alarm.

{% quote "A local in the pub" %}

I'm telling you, the detective says these people just.. vanish! No trace left behind. Mark my words, those wicked folk from Theorston Thicket are behind this. If those disciples from ancient times are trying to bring him back from the dead..

The world would have a whole new set of problems if [Archmage Anvar](/npcs/archmageAnvar) returns...

{% endquote %}

### But where can I start?
Naturally, you cannot simply dive into these grand problems like {% tooltip "a headless chickon" %}In Hesli, we call chickens, chickons. Why? Because I think it's funny, that's why. :D{% endtooltip %} after your arrival in [Limani](/atlas/eshil/limani). However, that doesn't mean there's nothing for you to do. Here are a few pointers to help you get started on your journey in Eshil. 

- Many merchants from the Limani Supplies Dealer are getting robbed at broad daylight without any repercussions. The organisation doesn't have enough hands for fully protecting their merchants against this. They've grown so desperate that they hire fresh adventurers to guard their junior and minor merchants.

- [Barnabas Stripepaw](/npcs/barnabasStripepaw) has some errands to run but not enough time to do it himself. If you help him out at [the Wandering Lileon](/atlas/eshil/limani/wanderingLileon), he'll give you great rewards. They're relatively simple jobs, but when done well it could earn you the trust of the lileon, who might have more pressing issues that he only shares with those he trusts.

- [Ashish Ludak](/npcs/ashishLudak) requires some information from one of their informants who hasn't returned from their mission yet. If you can get the information to Ashish, they will reward you greatly. If done well, you might even earn their trust which could land you some more interesting quests.

### But I want explore a little first!
Totally understandable, a fresh world, a new city, it's only natural wanting to see what's out there before getting in the heat of things. There are plenty of small villages around [Limani](/atlas/eshil/limani), shrines and temples to explore and learn more about the religions that are intertwined in this multicultural area.

[Barnabas](/npcs/barnabasStripepaw) and [Ashish](/npcs/ashishLudak) have plenty connections and knowledge in and around the town. So, if you're looking for something specific, there's no harm in asking them for some directions.

## Technology and Magic
When trekking through Hesli, you will encounter various levels of technology and magic. Some communities are more advanced in one or the other while others just prattle on clinging to the old ways of life. 

Don't expect towering factories or steam-powered marvels. Most communities still rely on craftsmanship, simple machinery and tried-and-tested techniques. Steam exists, but only in rare circumstances, leaving most of Hesli with a technological feel somewhere between the Middle Ages and the very early Industrial Revolution.

As for magic, it.. exists. But don't expect excessive use of magic or immense power radiating through arcane energy. All-powerful mages are a rare treat to stumble upon, but by no means unheard of — fortunately, for they are quite the threat. Like technology, magic is most often used to lighten the burden of everyday life.

## I want to play! What character can I make?
There are a few playable species in Hesli. You might already be familiar with most of them by their name, but be sure to read through their articles regardless. There are some interesting twists to take into account.

### Elf
Recognisable by their slender build and long ears, elves are often regarded as graceful and reserved folk. Though the many elven lineages differ greatly in appearance and way of life, they all share one peculiar calling: to nurture all that lives. 

{% button "/encyclopaedia/races/elf", "m-right" %}Learn more about the Elves{% endbutton %}

### Dwarf
Loyalty to family and companions lies at the heart of every dwarf. Though their traditions and outlooks differ greatly from one lineage to another, every dwarf has a habit of tackling life's challenges in their own way. But if one thing brings them together, it is the beard.

{% button "/encyclopaedia/races/dwarf", "m-right" %}Learn more about the Dwarfs{% endbutton %}

### Gnome
Gnomes have a proper knack for technological innovation, and their ambition carries them far into the world. Perseverance defines them; giving up simply isn't part of who they are. There is always more than one way to leave your mark on the world.

{% button "/encyclopaedia/races/gnome", "m-right" %}Learn more about the Gnomes{% endbutton %}

### Halfling
Often overlooked because of their stature, halflings have a remarkable talent for making themselves feel at home wherever — and however long — they decide to settle. Beneath their cheerful demeanour lies a resilience that allows them to flourish anywhere in the world.

{% button "/encyclopaedia/races/halfling", "m-right" %}Learn more about the Halflings{% endbutton %}

### Human
Humans are known for their utter stubbornness and their unwavering determination: they laugh at the odds. They also have an uncanny tendency to thrive where others would falter. It shows most in their mentality, for they believe that everything is possible.

{% button "/encyclopaedia/races/human", "m-right" %}Learn more about the Humans{% endbutton %}

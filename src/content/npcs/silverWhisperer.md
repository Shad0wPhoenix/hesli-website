A most illustrous person currently leading the [Bosqinchi]() is best known for their eerie mastery of manipulation, hence why they got the name Silver Whisperer. No one knows what they look like, who they are, even what specie they are. Shrouded in mystery, the Silver Whisperer likes to keep their opponents and enemies guessing for their identity while they wreak havoc in their territory between [Limani](/atlas/eshil/limani) and [Matona](/atlas/eshil/matona).

{% quote "Silver Whisperer" %}

When you know what a person wants, you have found the way of moving them to your will.

{% endquote %}

## Mystery Manipulator
Using their most important asset of skill, the Silver Whisperer puppeteers their subjects to do their bidding howevery they see fit. In many cases, they use it to persuade people to give up their possessions for the good of their organisation. That is why the pillaging of villages isn't always reported to the authorities in Limani and Matona, most people willingly give their money away and there is next to no violence to coerce them into giving up their belongings.

However, there is one, more sinister, cause for which the Silver Whisperer makes use of their talents. [Bosqinchi]() is always actively recruiting people as many members seem to vanish at broad daylight never to be seen again. Therefore, while going through the many villages, they keep an eye out for useful candidates to recruit. Without much fuss, the Silver Whisperer can bring up to twenty people back to the organisation to strenghten their numbers once again.

Because no one knows their identity, not even the people of [Bosqinchi](), the Silver Whisperer is capable of going outside without being noticed or recognised. There have been speculations of who they can be, but concrete evidence has yet to show up to be certain.

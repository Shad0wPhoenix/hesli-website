So, you want to write species - I see - but how do you go about that? What if you'd interview your species from the lens of a traveller? Or some poor soul that happens to come across this weird looking, maybe human-esque, person? It might help you figure out what your species is to write your articles about them.

See it as a helpful nifty toolie. You don't need to answer all of them. That.. that's most likely too much, although not impossible. I'd say, take 2 or 3 questions per relevant category and try to answer those to start with. It is highly possible that these questions spawn entirely new articles and ideas and yeah.. that's also kind of the purpose, haha.

Ready for some questions? Here you go!

{% endquote %}

{% endcontainer %}

### Identity
1. If someone had never met your people before, what is the first thing you would want them to understand?
2. What is something outsiders always get wrong about your people?
3. What makes you proud to belong to your people?
4. What is something your people do that would seem strange to others?
5. What word would your people use to describe themselves?

### Everyday Life
6. Walk me through a normal day for you!
7. What is something you do every day that others might find unusual?
8. What is the first thing a child of your people learns?
9. What skill does every member of your community know?
10. What would a visitor notice within their first hour among your people?

### Environment
11. Why do your people live where they do?
12. What does your homeland provide that nowhere else can?
13. What part of your homeland would outsiders struggle with?
14. What part of your homeland could you never give up?

### Culture
15. What is considered a great achievement among your people?
16. What is considered shameful?
17. What do your people value more than anything?
18. What do your people argue about?
19. What tradition would you defend even if everyone else mocked it?
20. What tradition do you secretly dislike? (This could differ between young, adult, and elderly people)

### Relationships
21. Which other people understand you best?
22. Which other people misunderstand you most?
23. What do you admire about another (sub)species?
24. What do you secretly envy about another (sub)species?
25. What stereotype about another group makes you roll your eyes?

### Biology
26. How has your body changed the way you live?
27. What can your people do that others cannot?
28. What limitation has your body given you?
29. What do others find impressive about your physical traits?
30. What physical trait do your people consider beautiful?

### Fun Flavour
31. What insult would actually hurt your people?
32. What compliment would make someone proud?
33. What saying would every child know?
34. What joke do your people tell about themselves?
35. What is something an elder would say that a youngster would roll their eyes at?
36. What would someone find in your home?
37. What food would you miss most while travelling?
38. What smell reminds you of home?
39. What sound reminds you of your people?
40. What story does every child grow up hearing?

For a long time, the peoples of Hesli have lived in relative peace. Its major powers have set aside the majority of their differences, allowing people of all shapes and sizes to build lives alongside one another.

Each nation has problems of their own, but nothing out of the ordinary. Small groups of adventurers can obtain a reputation, good or bad, when they venture through the world and help out the people. One's reputation can help them in achieve their goals more easily, or work against them.

However, it seems like grander problems are arising at the horizon. People are disappearing around Theorston Thicket, suggesting an old magic is going its course. Around Limani whispers bring rumours of a secret brotherhood are at the roots of a political movement. A set of prying, watchful eyes can be felt in the back of the head. Whatever is coming, Hesli will not remain untouched forever.

### Reputation System
As adventurers wander through the world, they gain a reputation with different organisations, people, clans, nations, and more. They can use it to persuade someone to make a change, or to prepare for something in their favour. Of course, parties with a less than favourable reputation will have more trouble influencing the people. Adventurers get their reputation by the quests they do, and their attitude towards the people, to name two examples.
 
Curious to learn more? Read [this article](/reputation-system) for a detailed explanation!

## Current Focus in Worldbuilding
This worldbuilding project is.. well, it's not young anymore if you count years of existence. When you count years of actively working on it, then yes, it's relatively young! There's still a lot of active development going on but since it's also very much a hobby project, I don't always have the time and energy to put into Hesli as much as I'd like it to be.

Eventually, I hope to play one or more campaign in this world with my friend, but that's a hope I've had for the past four years and.. well, it's not there yet, haha. Life is very good at throwing proper curveballs at you when you least expect it.

At the moment, I'm mostly migrating articles into this space and going over them again. This also results in a _lot_ of articles that I ~need~ want to rewrite because I figured out some new shenanigans for them. So, this world, it will change over time! Sometimes faster than other times, but keep coming back and you should be finding some changes around there every now and then.

The tool I used to build this is a tool a build myself which means, there are likely new things coming to this place eventually. Think comments, like, following this world and other fun stuff.

Am I stretched thin with hobbies? Why yes, yes I am, and that's the beauty of it: I can rotate between everything as much as I want. I hope you'll find some enjoyment in this corner of the internet!
